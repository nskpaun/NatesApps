01 · What

What is Prometheus Worlds?

An open source world builder. A world has visuals, sound and motion. Today it does four things.

  1. Sentence to world. Generate a world from a natural language description.
  2. Photos to world. Digitally recreate a real world space from a photograph.
  3. Stitching. Put worlds side by side and interpolate the connection between the two.
  4. Export to Game Engine.

The key restriction is it needs to be able to run 100% locally on reasonable consumer hardware: specifically a Mac with 64GB of memory.

03 · Rules

  • One machine, 64 GB of memory max.
  • Open models only.
  • No accounts, no cloud, no analytics, no external apis.

04 · How to Help

The whole project is open source. You can contribute PRs or you can fork it and send us a link to your fork for us to pull in your improvements
