02 · How

What it does.

  1. Write it. Say what is in the place and how it looks. Two sentences will do.
  2. Wait. The Mac blocks it out, shapes the objects, paints it and trains the world. About half an hour.
  3. Walk in. W, A, S, D. Drag to look. Or tick Tour and sit back.
  4. Break it. Drop balls. They bounce off the furniture. Turn the lights out and carry a lamp.
  5. Listen. Wind, a creak, an engine going past, and slow music. The app makes the sound itself.
  6. Join them. Push rooms together. Doorways open where the walls meet. That is a dungeon.

03 · Limits

The truth.

  • It is rough up close. From across the room it holds.
  • Trees are solid shapes that bend. Single leaves do not move.
  • The car slides. Its wheels do not turn.
  • The model that lays out the room is small. Sometimes it gets the room wrong. Ask again.
  • Rooms from photos want sixty photos or more. Twenty is thin.
  • You need an Apple Silicon Mac. It was built on an M4 Pro with 64 GB.
